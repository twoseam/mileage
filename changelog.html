Changelog

What's new and what got better, newest at the top.

v4.17.2 Current May 22, 2026
  • Fixed: the retro stripe no longer leaves a dark gap below it. The background now extends past the bottom of the screen, so the wedge stays flush against the nav bar.
  • Pace trend chart: lower on the chart now means a lower pace number (faster pace at the bottom, slower at the top).
v4.17.1 May 21, 2026
  • Fixed: walks that auto-import from your phone now get the correct weather and temperature. They had been arriving with no weather, and a temperature that read far too cold — the phone's Celsius reading was being stored as Fahrenheit.
  • Fixed: the retro stripe now sits fully above the bottom navigation bar, instead of having its lower edge hidden behind it.
  • Fixed: the home-screen count-up numbers and growing bars now animate once when the page loads, instead of replaying every time fresh data arrives.
v4.17.0 May 18, 2026
  • New Trends tab: Pace, Heart Rate, Distance, Efficiency, Cadence, Pace-vs-Temp scatter, Active Days/Month and This-Year-vs-Last charts — dot plots with LOESS trend lines, time-window dropdowns, Walk/Run toggles, finger-scrubbing, anomaly filtering, and press-and-hold to reorder.
  • Home page rebuilt as a goal dashboard: a swipeable progress ring with ahead/behind pace, current-week bars, plus add-your-own goals (miles, days, streak, weekly/monthly) saved on your device.
  • Historical weather backfilled for ~570 past activities; conditions now shown as clean weather icons on activity cards and detail.
  • Stats chart reworked: Walk/Run legend toggles filter in place, bars grow/animate cleanly from the baseline, fixed scale so toggling doesn't bounce.
  • Activity detail relaid out (Avg HR · Pace · Time / Calories · Cadence · Steps / map / Start · End · Elevation / Temp · Conditions) with a substantial Notes section.
  • One-tap Sync button to pull the latest workout from Health Auto Export.

  • Tightened the spacing between the bottom navigation tabs.

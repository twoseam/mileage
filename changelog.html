MILEAGE
Changelog

What's new and what got better, newest at the top.

v4.2.0 Current May 18, 2026
  • Faster and sturdier: pages remember their last data and show it instantly while the latest loads behind the scenes, and the app keeps itself warm so it doesn't go to sleep.
  • The loading screen can never get stuck again.
  • Pull down from the top of any page to refresh and pull in the newest version of the app.
v4.1.0 May 18, 2026
  • Route maps: open a recent activity and tap the expand button to see where you actually walked or ran, drawn on a map in the app's own colors.
  • Tap again for a full-screen view.
v4.0.0 May 18, 2026
  • Your whole history is in: every walk and run back to 2019 imported from Apple Health — about 600 activities, with heart rate and GPS tracks.
  • Old Nike runs were folded in and de-duplicated, so nothing is doubled up.
  • Single GPS points are no longer stored when you log — the route map replaces them.
v3.5.0 May 18, 2026
  • Renamed to just Mileage — it's no longer tied to one year, and the wordmark is simpler.
  • The entry-page total is now labelled “2026 Miles” so it's clear it's this year's, not all-time.
v3.4.0 May 18, 2026
  • The Stats chart now opens on the month view.
  • The current bar shows your walk-vs-run split at a glance.
v3.3.0 May 18, 2026
  • New Recent list on the Stats page — your latest activities as cards.
  • Tap a card for the full detail: pace, time, duration, weather, and the shoe you wore.
v3.2.0 May 18, 2026
  • Light and dark mode — a toggle at the bottom of the menu. It remembers your choice.
v3.1.0 May 18, 2026
  • Added this changelog, with a version link in the footer and the menu.
v3.0.1 May 18, 2026
  • Fixed the spinning loading screen that could hang forever while the server was waking up. Now it always lets you through to the app.
v3.0.0 May 17, 2026
  • The app is now three pages instead of one — a page to log a walk, a page for your stats and charts, and a page for your shoes. Use the menu to move between them.
  • The Stats page got real charts you can swipe through and zoom in on, with numbers that count up.
  • New Shoes page: every pair you walk in, with a photo, the miles they've done, and how close they are to worn out. Retire a pair when you're finished with it.
  • Logging a walk is easier — pick the shoe by tapping its photo, add your time and pace, and mark it a walk or a run.
  • You can add the app to your phone's home screen and it opens like a normal app.
v2.0.0 May 7, 2026
  • Moved the whole thing onto its own faster site, with the striped look and the proper fonts.
  • It loads quicker now, especially the second time you open it.
  • Added two new stats: your longest single walk and your longest run of days in a row.
  • Added the loading bar, a little progress bar when you save, and a check that stops you logging a day that hasn't happened yet.
v1.1.0 May 3, 2026
  • If you log miles for a day that already has some, it now asks whether to replace the number or add to it. Before, it quietly doubled it up.
v1.0.0 Apr 28, 2026
  • The first working version: log a walk from your phone and see your total miles, how many days you've walked, how you're tracking against the goal, your current streak, and a six-week chart.
